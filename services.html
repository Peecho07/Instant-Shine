How we work

Four steps to a deep, clean finish.

Every package starts with the wash. Higher tiers add the steps that bring dark paint back to a mirror.

  1. 01

    Wash

    Hand wash and bug removal with deionized water, so nothing dries into a spot.

  2. 02

    Decontaminate

    A clay bar lifts the bonded grit and road tar a wash leaves behind.

  3. 03

    Correct

    Paint correction is machine polishing that removes swirl marks, haze and oxidation.

  4. 04

    Protect

    Wax, sealant or ceramic coating seals in the gloss and makes water bead off.
