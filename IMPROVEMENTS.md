# Instant Shine Auto Detailing: Website Demo

Built on spec by tsKaizen. All copy, prices, hours, photos and the testimonial come from the current instantshineautodetailing.com.

## What changed and why it brings in more customers

| Current site | New demo | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| No way to book online. Customers must call or email. | 30 second booking form on every screen, plus "Book this" on each package that prefills the service | People browsing at night or at work can book without calling. Every missed call is a missed job. |
| Prices buried in a long single column on a separate page | Pricing front and center, switchable by vehicle type (Car, Small SUV, Large SUV) and category (Motorcycles, RVs, Coaches, Boats) | Customers see their exact starting price in one tap, which builds trust before the first call. |
| The dark vehicle specialty is mentioned once in the middle of the services page | It's the headline | A clear specialty is what makes someone choose you over the next detailer on Google. |
| 20+ years of experience and the owner story are at the bottom of the homepage | Owner section with a timeline and the full background | Customers hire people. Showing David's range (dealerships, body shops, coaches, airplanes) justifies the price. |
| No link to Google reviews | "Read our reviews on Google" button and a 5.0 rating in the hero | Social proof at the moment of decision. |
| Leftover icon template instructions ("Try to keep strokes at 4px...") print as visible text in the footer on every page | Removed | Looks unfinished to customers. |
| No sticky contact on mobile | Call and Book buttons pinned to the bottom of the phone screen | Most detailing searches happen on phones. |
| Wix branding, 3 pages | Single fast page, no builder branding, structured data for Google | Loads faster, and helps Google show hours and phone number directly in search. |

## Technical notes

- Plain HTML, CSS and JS. No build step. Deploys to Netlify by drag and drop.
- Booking form uses Netlify Forms. Submissions show up in the Netlify dashboard and can email the owner.
- Photos are copied from the existing site into images/ and hosted with the demo, so it no longer depends on Wix.
- Recommended before launch: a business email on a custom domain to replace the Yahoo address.
